VALORANT Agents

Explore VALORANT agents, their roles, abilities and gameplay tips. Learn which agents fit different playstyles and team strategies.

Jett

Duelist

A highly mobile Duelist focused on aggressive plays, entry fragging and creating space for the team.

Raze

Duelist

A powerful Duelist who uses explosive abilities to clear areas and pressure opponents.

Sova

Initiator

An information-focused Initiator who can reveal enemy positions and support team attacks.

Omen

Controller

A Controller who uses smokes, teleportation and vision-blocking abilities to control the map.

Cypher

Sentinel

A defensive Sentinel specializing in surveillance, information gathering and site control.

Sage

Sentinel

A supportive Sentinel who can heal teammates, slow enemies and revive a fallen teammate.

Brimstone

Controller

A Controller who provides reliable smoke coverage and team support during site executions.

Skye

Initiator

An Initiator who provides information, flashes and healing support for her team.
