VALORANT Beginner Guide

New to VALORANT? Learn the basic mechanics, agents, weapons, economy, maps and strategies you need to start improving.

1. Understand the Objective

VALORANT is a tactical 5v5 shooter. Attackers generally try to plant the Spike and defend it, while defenders try to prevent the plant or defuse the Spike.

Beginner Tip: Don't focus only on getting eliminations. Understanding the round objective is just as important.

2. Learn the Agents

Each agent has unique abilities and belongs to a specific role.

  • Duelist
  • Initiator
  • Controller
  • Sentinel
Start with one or two agents instead of trying to learn every agent immediately.

3. Learn the Weapons

VALORANT has different weapons designed for different situations and budgets.

  • Pistols
  • SMGs
  • Shotguns
  • Rifles
  • Sniper Rifles
  • Machine Guns

4. Understand the Economy

Credits are used to purchase weapons, shields and abilities. Learning when to buy and when to save is an important part of competitive play.

Avoid spending all your credits every round without considering the next round.

5. Improve Your Aim

Good crosshair placement is one of the most important fundamentals for new players.

  • Keep your crosshair around head level.
  • Practice stopping before shooting.
  • Learn recoil patterns.
  • Use a comfortable sensitivity.
  • Practice regularly instead of changing settings constantly.

6. Learn the Maps

Map knowledge helps you understand common angles, rotations, sites and enemy positions.

Learn common callouts so you can communicate quickly with your teammates.

7. Communicate With Your Team

VALORANT is a team game. Give useful information such as enemy locations, damage dealt and Spike information.

Keep your communication short and clear.

8. Practice Consistently

Improvement takes time. Focus on one part of your gameplay at a time rather than trying to fix everything in a single session.
